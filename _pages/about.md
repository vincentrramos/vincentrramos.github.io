About
------

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Max Weber Institute](https://www.mwi.uni-heidelberg.de/en) at Heidelberg University  within the ERC-SOCDEBT project, led by Nora Waitkus. In this capacity, I work on projects around debt accumulation over the life course and across population subgroups. Concurrently, I am a visiting research fellow at the University of Southampton's [ESRC Centre for Population Change](https://www.cpc.ac.uk/research_programme/connecting_generations/#Current).

My broader research interests thread two domains: (i) social stratification and social demography and (ii) labor market institutions. I am specifically interested in workers' _socio-demographic responses_ to employment uncertainty and _mobilization responses_ to insufficiency and discontent. In doing so, I employ a range of quantitative methods (descriptive, quasi-experimental, and experimental designs) on both representative surveys and novel collected data, focusing on country-specific contexts. My research has been published in the European Sociological Review, the British Journal of Industrial Relations, Social Science Research, and Industrial Relations, among others.

I obtained my PhD in 2024 from the [DYNAMICS Program](https://www.sowi.hu-berlin.de/en/dynamics/about-dynamics) jointly based at the [Hertie School Berlin](https://www.hertie-school.org/en/) and [Humboldt University Berlin](https://www.hu-berlin.de/en).

Feel free to [reach out](mailto:vincent.ramos@mwi.uni-heidelberg.de) if any of these research strands interest you!
