About
------

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Southampton, within the [ESRC Centre for Population Change-Connecting Generations](https://www.cpc.ac.uk/research_programme/connecting_generations/#Current) Project, working on topics around employment uncertainty, life course, and intergenerational exchange. In the summer of 2026, I will be joining the [Max Weber Institute](soz.uni-heidelberg.de/en/) at Heidelberg University as a postdoctoral researcher within the ERC-SOCDEBT project.

My research interests thread two domains: (i) social stratification and social demography and (ii) labor market institutions. I employ a wide range of quantitative methods (descriptive, quasi-experimental, and experimental designs) on both representative surveys and novel collected data, focusing on country-specific contexts.

I obtained my PhD in 2024 from the [DYNAMICS Program](https://www.sowi.hu-berlin.de/en/dynamics/about-dynamics) based at the [Hertie School Berlin](https://www.hertie-school.org/en/) and [Humboldt University Berlin](https://www.hu-berlin.de/en).

Feel free to [reach out](mailto:v.ramos@southampton.ac.uk) if any of these research strands interest you!
